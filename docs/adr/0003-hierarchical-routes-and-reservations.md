# Hierarchical routes and space-time reservations

Long routes (flat estimate over 48 blocks) run on a region layer over the 16³ navigation tiles; short routes, escapes from a build designation, nearest-safe-pose searches, traffic detours and engineering stay on flat A*. A region record holds its poses, its in-region edges, its exits and one reverse distance field per exit. An exit is a run of crossings into one neighbouring region, joined while neighbouring crossings step to each other both ways on both sides and capped at 8, so a single middle representative keeps reachability exact. Exits are directed: a drop has no exit back up. Records are built on demand, sliced, and shared by every query waiting on the same tile. The layer reads geometry only, plus one static rule: a pose whose support block falls is not a region pose, because the live policy always rejects it. Water, hazards, build reservations and units stay live checks in refinement and execution.

Every region a route reads is built exactly, so an exhausted search proves the goal unreachable: policy can only remove routes. A cut by node limit, overflow, confinement, exclusion or memory stays `limited`, and a tile that changed between reads ends the query `stale`; the start and goal tiles are pinned when the query is made. Route searches are A* over exits, and group fields are one reverse search from a move order's destinations toward the boxes of its units' starts. Routes weight the heuristic by 1.25, which bounds a route at 1.25× the abstract optimum (group fields by 2 since round 2, see below); without a weight, rough terrain ties A* across whole rectangles of regions. Measured against exact flat A* on fuzzed hills, routed pairs cost 1.016× on average (p95 1.07, max 1.20) and reachability agreed on every pair. Units refine the route two segments ahead by descending the exit fields under the live policy, fall back to a region-confined search, exclude a failed exit and replan, and replan from where they stand when a segment still to walk changed.

The region store has a byte cap and evicts in insertion order. Records index their slots by column, and empty tiles share one frozen record. Build work is charged against the navigation budget at `BUILD_WEIGHT` steps per operation. The store is derived and not hashed, but operation counts depend on it, so replays are exact only from tick 0; a future snapshot or restore must rebuild the store deterministically, or hash it. Queries in flight, shared builds and units' routes are hashed.

Walking units reserve (cell, tick) pairs over a rolling 32-tick window, and units standing still hold their cells with no end. The first owner of a (cell, tick) keeps it. When a projection collides, the unit with the lower priority repairs it with a space-time search in a small box: it rejoins its route further on, or ends in a pocket that nobody needs past the window. Priority is the order's priority plus aging for ticks without progress, and ties rotate with the tick instead of favouring an id. Occupancy remains the source of truth for collision; the table plans.

Conflicts are resolved best right of way first, at most 8 per tick: higher aged priority, then a rotation that changes every 64 ticks, then the lower id. A unit takes the right of way from an idle owner or one already yielding to it and, once stuck (no new best distance for 20 ticks), from one of lower priority, provided the owner finds a refuge off its route; when only the stuck unit has a refuge, the roles reverse. Otherwise an idle owner, or an idle unit next to either of them, walks off the route within 16 blocks. A working unit is never displaced, and a unit that yielded keeps yielding for 200 ticks. Repairs prefer the right-hand lane. After 160 stuck ticks the earlier detour, sidestep and traffic-block rules take over. Known limit: dense opposing flows still jam (30 against 30 in a 128-long two-wide corridor); 20 against 20 passes, slowly.

Round 2 (2026-10-12). Random ticks wet and dry sand nearly every tick, so a found route is stale only when a region it crosses changed and a group field never goes stale (units price their way on from the current record); an exhausted search still proves nothing unless every region it read is unchanged. A unit recovers locally on its route's own costs instead of planning anew, and the record of a changed tile is rebuilt on the refinement budget. Regions are built ahead in the background with the operations a tick leaves unused (rings around units, then every tile by index), the store cap is 64 MiB and the prebuild stops at 90% of it; a build a query leaves is kept and finished by the next reader or the prebuild. Records live as long as some route or field holds them: eviction only means another query rebuilds. Exits keep their whole run of crossings, and a unit crosses at the first one it reaches on its way to the middle, so groups keep lanes through a portal.

Searches add a landmark (ALT) bound to the flat one: eight tiles on the world's edge, each with every exit's cost to it from an exhaustive reverse search over the region graph, computed by the prebuild once every tile is built. The tables are exact for the records they read, the bound skips any unknown value so the heuristic stays finite (exhaustion still proves unreachable), and a solid edit retires them until they are computed again. Sand that gets wet adds poses, so in and next to tiles where the falls rule has ever changed the tables read poses on falling blocks as standable, which keeps them a lower bound through later flips there; the first flip in a new tile retires them too. A recompute starts only after 200 ticks without such changes, and background work rests while there is no unit and no query. Dry sand that has never changed stays a barrier, so the tables still see the sand detours they exist for. Group fields weight their heuristic by 2 and routes by 1.25; on the warm crowd world both stay within mean 1.10 and p95 1.25 of exact flat A* with reachability agreeing. A flat search whose blocked set of bodies cut an edge proves nothing and ends limited.

A unit that a traffic block stops keeps its settled route or group field and carries on from where it stands; it never plans a route of its own for traffic. A unit leads its followers in a queue only while it is on its way: one blocked, or waiting for a search, shields no one. Measured at 1,000 units the cooperation layer does not hold the scale targets (see the 5b report): groups merge into single-file lines and the waiting units cost most of the tick.

Round 3 (2026-10-13). The region layer reads water as geometry. A pose whose head cell holds liquid, or whose feet stand in a spring or a liquid other than water, is no region pose; the live policy refuses the same poses, so a route never crosses what a unit refuses (before, water-blind routes crossed water the live policy refused, and units looped there for good). Units wade: they enter water only with the head dry, and once their feet are wet they carry on through water up to 1.5 blocks, so a lake rising a little does not strand them. A unit caught deeper wades out to the nearest safe pose, and a unit wading off the region layer first wades to a region pose nearer its goal. A step onto a wet pose costs 8 more in planning (about three steps; relaxed records charge nothing, so landmark tables stay a lower bound), so routes keep to dry land unless going round is much longer. Liquid changes reach the region layer per tile 200 ticks after the first one, in one revision bump, because water at river edges flips many times a tick and per-flip bumps restarted every build and search over it. After the first complete prebuild pass, the prebuild rebuilds only tiles changed since, oldest first, at most `prebuildCap` operations a tick once the world is warm. A unit queued behind a unit on its way sleeps: it keeps its safety checks and its counters, skips decisions, and wakes when that unit's cells change, its tile changes, a command arrives, physics needs it or 16 ticks pass; cooperation wakes a sleeper before it touches its plan. Units that are not moving, working or idle take decisions under a FIFO budget of `decisionCap` per tick. While a unit with nothing to walk waits for its route or group field, repairs get an eighth of the leftover budget. Measured at 100 units (see the round-3 report), arrival and correctness gates improved (mass 50% to 100%, no false unreachable) but the workers' share, the first action of group orders and the head-on bottleneck did not reach their targets.
